About this page

This is a personal, non-commercial fan project — a way to browse the wonderful things Tim Ferriss shares each week in his 5-Bullet Friday newsletter. Everything here was gathered from 561 editions between July 2015 and August 2026. All recommendations, and all the credit, belong to Tim and 5-Bullet Friday. If you enjoy what you find here, the best thing you can do is subscribe to the newsletter and get it straight from the source.

Nothing is collected from you on this page: there are no accounts, no tracking and no email sign-up here — the Subscribe buttons simply send you to Tim's official page.

A note on accuracy

This archive was assembled with the help of AI, working from the newsletter's back-catalogue. That means it may contain mistakes — a wrong title, a mismatched cover, a misattributed author or the occasional broken link. Any such errors are entirely mine and are not the responsibility of Tim Ferriss or the 5-Bullet Friday newsletter. If you spot something off, please treat the official newsletter as the source of truth.
