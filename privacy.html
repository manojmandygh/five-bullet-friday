Privacy & data

Privacy note

This is an independent, non-commercial fan page. It is designed to collect and store as little as possible about you — in normal use, nothing personal is collected or stored by this site. This note explains exactly what happens.

We don't collect your details

There are no accounts, no contact forms, and no analytics or advertising trackers on this site. We do not build a profile of you or sell any data, because we hold none.

The newsletter signup

The "Subscribe" button sends you to Tim Ferriss's official 5-Bullet Friday signup at tim.blog/friday. Any email address you enter is submitted on Tim's own page, under his privacy policy — it is never sent to, or stored by, this site.

Cover artwork (third-party requests)

To show book covers and album art, your browser requests images and titles directly from public catalogues as you scroll:

  • Google Books API — for book covers and links.
  • Apple iTunes Search API — for album artwork and links.
  • Google Fonts — for typography.

These requests are made by your browser to those services and are subject to their own privacy policies. This site does not add identifiers to them.

Local storage

To avoid re-fetching the same cover repeatedly, your browser caches the public cover-image URLs in your device's local storage. This is a functional performance cache only — it holds no personal information and never leaves your device. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

Hosting & security

The site is a static page served over HTTPS via Netlify, whose servers keep standard, short-lived request logs for security and delivery. A strict content-security policy and modern security headers are applied.

Your choices & contact

Because no personal data is collected here, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For anything related to the 5-Bullet Friday newsletter itself, please contact Tim Ferriss's team via tim.blog. For questions about this fan page, reach the maintainer, Manoj (@manojmandy).

This unofficial tribute is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Tim Ferriss. Cover artwork and titles are shown for identification and reference only, and remain the property of their respective owners.
